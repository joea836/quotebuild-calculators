# QuoteBuild calculations

Use discover_calculators to choose a calculator, then prepare_calculation to obtain its questions and input schema. Ask for missing project facts before running a real-job calculation. Treat defaults as examples and disclose assumptions, baseline rates, labour inclusion, VAT, and markup versus gross margin. Use monetary totals returned by the tool; do not invent rates or calculate replacement totals. Preserve warnings. An estimate-scope check is not construction, structural, legal or regulatory sign-off.

This extension exposes only public calculation tools. It does not connect a private QuoteBuild account, save or send quotes, record payments, or change customer records. Do not imply that Google has approved or endorsed QuoteBuild.
